## 1. Digital Habits & Online Safety

### Spotting Phishing & Suspicious Links
* **Urgency Signals:** Scammers frequently use artificial panic ("Your account will be closed in 10 minutes!", "You won 1,000,000 FCFA, claim now!").
* **Verification Rule:** Never share passwords, one-time SMS verification codes (OTP), or parents' bank details over WhatsApp or unknown links.
* **Strong Password Hygiene:** Use passphrases with letters, numbers, and symbols that are easy to remember but impossible for computers to guess.

### Artificial Intelligence & Algorithms
* AI models predict answers based on training data patterns.
* Always cross-check important facts and cultivate critical thinking.

## 2. Trade, Barter, and Household Economics

### The Principle of Mutual Barter
* Before coins and banknotes existed, humans bartered goods directly: a bag of rice for three fish, or a math textbook for a chemistry notebook.
* **Double Coincidence of Wants:** Barter requires that person A has what person B wants, and person B has what person A needs.
* In modern community book matchmakers (like Relay), smart software coordinates this matching automatically across whole school parent networks!

### Opportunity Cost & Smart Budgeting
* **Opportunity Cost:** The value of the next best choice that you give up when making a decision.
  - If you spend 2,000 FCFA on candy, the opportunity cost is the science notebook you cannot buy.
* **Percentages & Discounts:**
  - 50% discount means half price (`Price / 2`).
  - 25% discount means one-quarter off (`Price * 0.75`).
  - If a textbook costs 4,000 FCFA and is discounted 25%, you save 1,000 FCFA and pay 3,000 FCFA.

## 1. Fractions, Ratios, and Proportions

### Understanding Fractions as Equal Parts
A fraction represents part of a whole or part of a set of items:
* **Numerator:** How many parts you have.
* **Denominator:** How many equal parts make up the whole.
* **Visual Pizza Analogy:**
  - A pizza cut into 8 equal slices: each slice is `1/8`.
  - If you eat 3 slices, you have eaten `3/8` of the pizza, and `5/8` remains.
  - `4/8` simplifies to `1/2` (half the pizza).

### Fractions of a Group (Discrete Quantities)
To find a fraction of a whole number:
* Formula: `(Total Quantity ÷ Denominator) × Numerator`
* Example: Amadou has 12 oranges and gives `1/3` to Fatima.
  - `12 ÷ 3 = 4`
  - `4 × 1 = 4` oranges.

## 2. Pre-Algebra & Linear Equations

### Balancing the Scale
An algebraic equation is like a balanced two-pan balance scale:
* Whatever operation you perform on the left side, you must perform on the right side.
* **Equation:** `2x + 6 = 14`
  - Step 1: Subtract 6 from both sides -> `2x = 8`
  - Step 2: Divide both sides by 2 -> `x = 4`

### Negative Numbers & Order of Operations (PEMDAS)
* Parentheses -> Exponents -> Multiplication/Division (left to right) -> Addition/Subtraction (left to right).
* Multiplying two negative numbers produces a positive number: `(-3) × (-4) = +12`.
